# Themes

**How this one narrow game connects to bigger open questions about agents, and who is working on each.**

The game is narrow on purpose. It asks one thing: when results drop, can an agent tell a shock that will pass from a switch to a new normal? That skill sits inside several larger questions people are already working on. Each row below is a claim to test, not a finding. What the game gives up, and which rows check that it carries outside itself, is in [limits](../design/limits.md).

The main inspiration is Epoch AI's question of whether agents can learn on the fly, getting better at a task by doing it. Our game isolates one slice of that. Learning a lesson is not enough when the world moves, because the lesson can go stale. The Shock then switch scenario is the sharpest case: an agent that rightly learnt "dips recover" has to notice when that stops being true ([scenarios](../design/scenarios.md)).

| **Learning on the fly** | Epoch AI: [EBR-bench](https://epoch.ai/publications/earthborne-rangers-benchmark) (Ou and Burnham 2026) and [its update](https://epoch.ai/publications/ebr-bench-update). | **METR's time horizon** | METR: [time horizons](https://metr.org/time-horizons), from [Kwa et al. 2025](measuring-agents.md#metrs-time-horizon-kwa-et-al-2025-success-against-task-length) | METR's tasks rarely change unless the agent acts, and they list that as a limit. A world that moves under the plan is the part they leave out | Whether a model's score here explains why it sits above or below the time horizon trend |
| **Exploring what you don't use** | Microsoft Research: [Krishnamurthy et al. 2024](learning-from-results.md#krishnamurthy-et-al-2024-a-summarised-history-and-exploration). Epoch's EBR-bench | LLMs explore too little, and on EBR-bench agents try few of the possible decks. Here a sleeper can rise past the leader, and the agent sees nothing of it until it bets on it ([what the agent sees](../design/mechanics.md#what-the-agent-sees)) | How often agents look at the field, read from their bets, and whether looks rise after a dip |
| **Does thinking longer help?** | Epoch AI: [EBR-bench](https://epoch.ai/publications/earthborne-rangers-benchmark) | Epoch finds more inference compute "doesn't overcome every capability deficiency" | Whether scores rise with reasoning. | **Forecasters versus readers** | Mistral: [Timestral](https://github.com/mistralai/timestral). | **Agent handoffs** | Anthropic: [Effective harnesses for long-running agents](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/effective-harnesses-for-long-running-agents) (2025). [Fresh Memory, Stale Plans 2026](past-in-the-prompt.md#fresh-memory-stale-plans-2026-a-current-fact-under-an-old-plan) | Long-running agents hand over through progress files and summaries when their context runs out. Our agent starts every round fresh with only its record, so each round is a small handoff ([tools](../design/interface.md#tools)) | Whether a record pre-filled by a baseline plays like the agent's own ([warm-up cell](../../TODO.md#pilot)), and whether notes help ([notepad check](../pilot/checks.md#notepad-and-compute)) |
| **Test awareness** | Apollo Research and OpenAI: [Schoen et al. 2025](measuring-agents.md#schoen-et-al-2025-suspecting-a-test-changes-behaviour). Here suspicion would show as extra looks or early moves | Whether the models that most often say they are being tested lose most in the No change and One shock scenarios |
| **Reward hacking** | METR: [Recent frontier models are reward hacking](https://metr.org/blog/2025-06-05-recent-reward-hacking) (2025). On EBR-bench, GPT-6 Astra hit a perfect score with a card combination that sidesteps the game's time pressure | Transcript review for misuse of the retry on bad bets and of the `compute` sandbox in the [notepad check](../pilot/checks.md#notepad-and-compute) |
| **Deference to a confident source** | Anthropic: [Sharma et al. 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.13548) on sycophancy. This is skin sensitivity, measured once more than one skin runs ([spread](../design/scoring.md#spread)) |

| **Strategy and incumbents** | [Allen and McDonald 2026](worlds-that-change.md#allen-and-mcdonald-2026-a-strategy-class-simulation).
