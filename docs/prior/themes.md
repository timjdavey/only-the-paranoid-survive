# Themes

**How this one narrow game connects to bigger open questions about agents, and who is working on each.**

The main inspiration is Epoch AI's question of whether agents can learn on the fly, getting better at a task by doing it with only their own notes carried between attempts. This game isolates one slice of that: noticing that a lesson in those notes has gone stale because the world moved. It serves **part of the wider field** ([design principles](../design/requirements.md#design-principles)). Each row is a claim to test, not a finding.

| Theme | Who works on it | The link | What we'd check |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Learning on the fly** | Epoch AI: [EBR-bench](https://epoch.ai/publications/earthborne-rangers-benchmark) (Ou and Burnham 2026) and [its update](https://epoch.ai/publications/ebr-bench-update). Dwarkesh Patel: [The next big breakthrough will be AIs learning on the job](https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/the-next-paradigm) (2026) | Epoch has agents replay a hard board game 10 or 30 times, keeping notes between games, and finds little or no improvement. Our game is the case where the right lesson changes partway through, so learning it once is not enough | Whether the models that do well here are the ones that start improving on EBR-bench, and whether they rewrite their notes after the change |
| **METR's time horizon** | METR: [time horizons](https://metr.org/time-horizons), from [Kwa et al. 2025](measuring-agents.md#metrs-time-horizon-kwa-et-al-2025-success-against-task-length) | METR measures how long a task an agent can finish, and credits part of the rise in horizons to agents adapting to their own mistakes. Noticing that the world has moved under a plan is that skill, isolated | Whether a model's discernment explains why it sits above or below the time horizon trend. See [Why older models and a general ability check](../design/decisions.md#why-older-models-and-a-general-ability-check) |
| **The same headline form** | METR's time horizon. Epoch AI: [Capabilities Index](https://epoch.ai/eci), one general ability score built from many benchmarks | The full study's discernment copies METR's recipe: a curve of success against a natural unit, read at a set level and plotted against release date | Discernment, METR's horizon and Epoch's index side by side on one chart |
| **Exploring what you don't use** | Microsoft Research: [Krishnamurthy et al. 2024](learning-from-results.md#krishnamurthy-et-al-2024-a-summarised-history-and-exploration). [BanditBench](learning-from-results.md#banditbench-nie-et-al-2025-in-context-exploration) (Nie et al. 2025). Epoch's EBR-bench | LLMs explore too little, and on EBR-bench agents try few of the possible decks. Here the option that gets better pays nothing until the agent bets on it | Whether agents ever experiment on the option they ignore, read from the curiosity diagnostic in [Failure measures](../design/measure-failure.md#diagnostics) |
| **Does thinking longer help?** | Epoch AI: [EBR-bench](https://epoch.ai/publications/earthborne-rangers-benchmark) | Epoch finds more inference compute "doesn't overcome every capability deficiency" | Whether scores rise with thinking budget, in the full study's reasoning sweep ([TODO](../../TODO.md#full-study) step 4) |
| **Agent handoffs** | Anthropic: [Effective harnesses for long-running agents](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/effective-harnesses-for-long-running-agents) (2025). [STALE 2026](past-in-the-prompt.md#stale-2026-knowing-a-memory-is-stale-and-acting-on-it). [Fresh Memory, Stale Plans 2026](past-in-the-prompt.md#fresh-memory-stale-plans-2026-a-current-fact-under-an-old-plan) | Long-running agents hand over through progress files, summaries and memory files when their context runs out. The game is a controlled takeover of a record, and the agent's notes are the memory file it writes for itself | Whether an inherited plan, with its voice, changes the play, in the [inherited plan extension](../design/future.md#what-the-agent-knows-before-it-starts) |
| **Test awareness** | Apollo Research and OpenAI: [Schoen et al. 2025](measuring-agents.md#schoen-et-al-2025-suspecting-a-test-changes-behaviour). MATS and Apollo: [Needham et al. 2025](measuring-agents.md#needham-et-al-2025-telling-a-test-from-real-use). UK AI Security Institute: [alignment evaluation case study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00788) (Souly et al. 2026) | Frontier models increasingly say they think they are being tested, which can change what they do | How often they say so here, and whether it drives experimenting. See [Why the game is hard to game](../design/decisions.md#why-the-game-is-hard-to-game) |
| **Reward hacking** | METR: [Recent frontier models are reward hacking](https://metr.org/blog/2025-06-05-recent-reward-hacking) (2025). Anthropic: [MacDiarmid et al. 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.18397). Epoch AI: [EBR-bench update](https://epoch.ai/publications/ebr-bench-update) | Agents exploit the scoring when they can. On EBR-bench, GPT-6 Astra hit a perfect score with a card combination that sidesteps the game's time pressure | Transcript review for tool misuse and steering text in the notes |
| **Deference to a confident source** | Anthropic: [Sharma et al. 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.13548) on sycophancy. Google DeepMind: [Kumaran et al. 2025](past-in-the-prompt.md#kumaran-et-al-2025-whose-first-answer-it-was) | A plan written by someone else is an authority, like a user who sounds sure. Sycophancy work asks whether models defer too long. The game removes that author by default | Whether a plan written in a more confident voice slows the switch, in the [inherited plan extension](../design/future.md#what-the-agent-knows-before-it-starts) |
| **Belief updating in context** | [Schubert et al. 2024](past-in-the-prompt.md#schubert-et-al-2024-asymmetric-belief-updating). The human result: [Chambon et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-020-0919-5) | Schubert et al. found Claude v1.3 learns more from good news than bad about its own choices, but symmetrically about choices someone else made | Whether the agent discounts losses on its notes once it has staked on them itself, so a read record stops counting as someone else's choice |
