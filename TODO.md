## Full study

Once the proof of concept has run on a few models and the error bars hold:

1. **Run the five-amplitude grid** on about 30 seeds a model, with extra runs at the amplitudes where a model starts to miss.
2. **Fit discernment** and publish it at both levels, 50% tracked and 80% quoted, with the catch rate, d′ and the CUSUM dot beside it. Publish each threshold also as the evidence a round carries at that amplitude, so a later, harder edition sits on the same axis.
3. **Add the later kinds of change**, coupling, gain and observation, one at a time, each with its own baseline check from step 1 and run once on a few models before it joins the grid.
4. **Sweep reasoning.** Run each model at several thinking budgets and plot the score against them.
5. **Run every model on release**, and back-fill older models, so the line over time has more than a few points.
6. **Run the real-task check**: one real agentic task with a stale handoff note, on two or three models, read by hand, and compare the ranking with the game's. See [future work](docs/design/future.md#whether-it-holds-outside-our-game).
7. **Replay a few runs** to see how much of a score is the model's own randomness, and read every transcript by hand rather than a sample.

Two claims hold up as written. README's "Most tests count only being too slow" is fair: Set-shifting and Bazaar have no-change controls, but neither counts a false alarm against the agent in its score. And Wang, Ward and Zhang's lose-shift numbers (0.035 to 0.241 for models, 0.495 for people) match their Table 2.

Two design choices gain support. [Kumaran](docs/prior/past-in-the-prompt.md#kumaran-et-al-2025-whose-first-answer-it-was) found the ownership effect vanished when the first answer was credited to another model, which is why the notes are now the agent's own rather than a previous agent's plan. And in [Allen and McDonald](docs/prior/worlds-that-change.md#allen-and-mcdonald-2026-a-strategy-class-simulation) newer frontier models scored lower than older ones, which backs keeping an older model in the pilot and warns that the line need not rise.
